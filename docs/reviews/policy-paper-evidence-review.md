## R1: limit the stage-5 restriction to discretionary changes

The stage-5 title, “Increase support only after a joint review”, can be read to delay emergency relief or an already-authorized adjustment to the basic payment. That reading conflicts with the immediate relief in stage 3 and the basic-payment rule in Section 6. Limit this condition to discretionary increases or an expansion of the permanent promise. Keep the review requirement for those choices. This is a sequencing correction, not a change to the evidence.

## R2: distinguish annual surplus units from a calendar date

The reserve example says restoration takes six and a half years. Annual totals establish 6.5 normal-year surpluses, but do not establish the within-year receipt path. If each surplus is added at year-end, seven complete normal years are needed to reach 26. State the unit or the timing assumption. The surrounding warning about cash dates is sound; this change aligns the sentence with it.
