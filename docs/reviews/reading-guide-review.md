# Independent AI review of the reading guide

**Decision:** Ready for release as a guide. No blocking error found.

**Review completed:** 2026-10-12T17:19:01.215733Z

**Reviewer:** A separate AI agent, `/root/large_load`, which did not write the guide. This is an AI review, not human expert review or journal peer review.

I compared every study summary with the relevant sections of the ten local complete-study manuscripts. I checked the guide's numerical examples and the stated limits. I also checked that each study folder has a protocol, source records, results and AI review files. This review does not repeat all ten analyses, recheck every external source, or verify remote publication.

## Findings

| Study | Manuscript locations checked | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| [S004](../../studies/2026-10-11-post-labor-floor/paper.md) | Sections 5.1 and 6 | The base case passes the reproducible-goods floor and misses the essential-service floor. Investment uses current resources. |
| [S005](../../studies/2026-10-11-dividend-reserves/paper.md) | Sections 3–5 and 7 | Cash cover depends on the largest gap and the settlement date. Asset sales can reduce later receipts. Synthetic pass rates are not probabilities. |
| [S006](../../studies/2026-10-11-automation-imports/paper.md) | Sections 3.2, 5.1 and 6 | Gross levy receipts, net transfer funding and national consumption differ. More transfer funding can accompany less consumption. |
| [S007](../../studies/2026-10-11-benefit-access/paper.md) | Sections 4–5 and 7–8 | Eligible arrears and ineligible advances have different funding needs. A stable queue can have continuing losses. Payment timing assumes a working payment system. |
| [S008](../../studies/2026-10-11-public-capital-acquisition/paper.md) | Sections 3–5 and 7–8 | The purchase needs a price and finance. Expected gains do not ensure protection in each state. Returns can offset weak taxes without creating free wealth. |
| [S009](../../studies/2026-10-11-international-dividend/paper.md) | Sections 3–6 and 9 | Payment incentives depend on timing and future cooperation. Persistent adverse states can weaken them. A financed deposit can help, but its cost can prevent participation. |
| [S010](../../studies/2026-10-11-public-ownership-control/paper.md) | Sections 3.3, 4–6 and 7 | The three funds have different purposes and powers. Review routes must match the decision. The paper does not test whether the proposed design prevents capture. |
| [S011](../../studies/2026-10-11-household-debt/paper.md) | Sections 3–6 and Table 1 | The guide keeps the family unit, fixed-payment assumption and constructed debt measure. The central figures and their limits agree with the paper. |
| [S012](../../studies/2026-10-12-redistribution-audit/paper.md) | Sections 1–6 | The comment examines four claims in one source version. It separates two corrections from two conditional results. The income example is correct. |
| [S013](../../studies/2026-10-12-supplier-continuity/paper.md) | Sections 3–7 and 9 | The selected four records support a candidate screen. Model, platform, buyer access and support dates can exclude a pair. All 24 operational-readiness cells remain unknown. |

The guide's $2,000 transfer and $1,000 threshold match S011. Its 22.9 percent is the paper's 22.90 percent rounded to one decimal place. In that experiment, remaining resources are bounded at zero. Thus every family passes the gross test by construction. The guide calls this a conditional accounting result and does not turn it into a forecast of hardship or default.

I checked the S012 example with exact arithmetic: the gaps before and after the addition are both 2; the ratios are 3 and 2. This example illustrates relative inequality. It does not claim to include the cost of funding the addition.

The prose is suitable for a reader who has not read the papers. Each section has a clear question and links to the relevant manuscript. The guide retains the distinction between model results, survey accounting and document audits. It states the conditional AI premise, the absence of human peer review, and the limits of combining the papers. I found no unsupported claim of a joint policy result. No change is required for this release.
