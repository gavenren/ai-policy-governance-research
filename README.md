## Research standard

Each study will include its question, closest prior research, bounded contribution, legal-status table, evidence cutoff, source retrieval records and hashes, reproducible methods, results including negative findings, and independent critical review by another AI agent. AI review is not human or external peer review. Publication quality is a target, not a certification.

## Structure

- `docs/` — programme protocol, study queue, and publication checks.
- `templates/` — reusable study protocol and report structure.
- `studies/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/` — study-specific protocol, sources, data, code, outputs, paper, and review.
- `scripts/` — local validation and provenance utilities.

Work labelled **protocol** or **working notes** is unfinished. A **complete-study draft** requires completed analyses and documented quality checks; it is still an AI-assisted draft.

## Disclosure and scope

Codex assists with research, coding, drafting, and review. No human review, journal submission, acceptance prospect, institutional endorsement, or exhaustive novelty proof is implied. Only public-source evidence that may be redistributed is included; otherwise source metadata, retrieval instructions, and permissible excerpts are used.
