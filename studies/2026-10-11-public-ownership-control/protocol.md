# S010 research protocol

**State:** Working notes.
**Recorded:** 2026-10-11T21:24:48+00:00

## Question

When a public investment fund supports income outside paid work, which bodies control the payment, the portfolio vote, and a request to change either decision? What would an AI dividend proposal have to add?

## Design and scope

A selected comparison of Alaska Permanent Fund/Dividend institutions, Norway GPFG, and New Zealand Superannuation Fund. Their purposes differ. The case choice is purposive: a dividend, a general fiscal fund, and a pension reserve. The unit is a decision-authority relation tied to a provision, not a country score. No claim of representativeness or causal impact will be made. Existing cases are institutional analogues, not actual post-labor economies.

Code (1) ownership and individual claim, (2) spending authority, (3) investment and corporate voting authority, (4) permitted government direction, (5) oversight, and (6) scope and remedy of an identified challenge channel. Test three questions: a lower payment, a contested portfolio vote, and a complaint sent to the fund. Keep political influence, operational discretion, individual appeal, and a binding remedy separate. Missing evidence is unknown, not absence. No index or total quality score.

## Sources and stopping rule

Use current official instruments and administering bodies, with version checks and short public extracts. Read Truman's scoreboard, Santiago Principles and Bruenig's fund proposal beyond abstracts. Follow the latter to direct-voting alternatives if feasible. Finish at three cases and these six dimensions when each pivotal relation has inspectable primary evidence. If a current legal claim cannot be verified, narrow it to an official description or historical holding and mark the gap. Do not add cases to produce a preferred conclusion.

## Planned validation

Check all coded rows for source and provision links, duplicated keys, evidence wording, instrument versions and interpretation status. Generate a case matrix and decision-route table. Run sensitivity classifications that treat unknown challenge powers as potentially available; test whether positive documented separations still hold. Independent AI review by another agent is required before complete-study status. No human coding or inter-rater estimate is planned.

## Timing and changes

This protocol follows exploratory source searches and reading. It is not a preregistration. Norway's November 2025 temporary ethics rules were found before coding. They replace the outdated permanent exclusion procedure for the main comparison. Official HTML acquisition has yielded empty or blocked responses for some sites; such captures will be excluded and logged, never treated as valid law.
