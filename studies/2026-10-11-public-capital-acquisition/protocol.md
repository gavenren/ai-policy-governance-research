# S008 exploratory protocol

Recorded during development, 2026-10-11. This is not a prospective registration. State: Working notes.

Question: what can a fair, debt-financed public equity purchase contribute to a guaranteed social dividend, and what additional fiscal capital is required?

The model has two dates, positive finite-state prices, separately stated physical probabilities, nonnegative asset cash flows, outside net fiscal receipts, and a bounded equity share. Purchase money reaches sellers. Debt is repaid once. Initial capital funds the purchase or a safe deposit. Final asset cash includes all terminal value.

The output is an exact initial-contribution certificate, a price-value identity, a max-min ownership choice, and explicit default repricing. Six synthetic cases cover a risk premium, a fiscal hedge, common losses, three states, weaker tax receipts, and a safe asset. No empirical parameters or random samples are used.

The base question was selected before coding. The fiscal-hedge comparison was added during development after discussion with the coordinating agent. It is exploratory. A separate reviewer who did not suggest that extension performs critical review.

Sources include close public research on AI ownership and debt-financed wealth funds. Dynan et al. and Korinek/Lockwood original captures are reused with original dates and hashes. Belda et al. was acquired and read beyond its abstract. The Chien/Du/Lustig PDF request failed; it is a discovery lead only, not manuscript evidence. The paper explicitly acknowledges established budget and asset-pricing logic and does not claim mathematical priority.

Nine tests check purchase accounting, minimal contributions, value reconciliation, interior and boundary optima, default pricing, probability changes, and a separate fine share grid. Complete stated cases are retained. The stopping rule is completion of this bounded model, evidence package, separate review, and release checks. No expansion or empirical claim is planned.

State remains Working notes until blocking review issues are resolved. The coordinator records the final commit and verifies remote publication.
