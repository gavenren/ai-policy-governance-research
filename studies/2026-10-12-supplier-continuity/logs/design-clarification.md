# Design clarification

Recorded 2026-10-12T10:57:38Z, before coding and enumeration.

The Google card's retirement footnote concerns support on its cloud platform. Therefore the later-date scenario requires continued support, and excludes the listed deployment if the announced retirement takes effect. It is not a prediction of endpoint shutdown. The same date is not applied to the separate Gemini API.

The four-model corpus is unchanged. A supplier-authored public procurement listing (I1) and official UK AI guidance (I2) were added as context. They test whether the purchase record distinguishes seller, model maker, cloud operator, capacity, and service-level requirements. They are not additional deployment observations and are not evidence of a public contract award.

The public listing allows a source-based distinction between catalog access and committed capacity. The guidance's NHS cases distinguish an attempted local model that failed the task-quality bar from a deployed service assessed for load. These facts are used to explain missing readiness evidence. No performance rate is transferred to the four model rows.

The two-by-two label counterexample alone is not claimed as a research innovation. The deliverable is an explicitly selected case audit linking those labels, the support horizon, and the limits of the public purchase evidence. It remains small and has no population inference.
