# Study design and decision record

Design record for the complete-study draft. Recorded 11 October 2026. This is an exploratory design record, not a preregistration.

The user authorized one research paper on economic governance when AI can outcompete human labor in all fields. This instruction authorizes S004. It does not resume the other paused studies or the continuing research programme.

The question is whether a cash transfer can meet a two-good consumption floor with public revenue limits and private ownership of scarce assets. The model is a conditional scenario. All numerical inputs are synthetic. The stopping rule is a complete manuscript, reproducible analysis, source ledger, and separate AI review of central results. No country dataset, survey, or causal estimate is planned.

## Decisions and changes

- 2026-10-11, before analysis: choose a joint fiscal and real-access test. Search primary literature with several formulations. Treat universal novelty as unproved.
- During source review: identify close 2026 work by Song and Nayebi. Narrow the contribution to an explicit two-group floor threshold and investment tradeoff. Do not claim that scarcity or rent-funded transfers are new.
- During model design: reject a fixed outside-demand approximation. Include essential rents in owners' income so both markets close.
- Before the executed grid: choose the input ranges in `data/design.json`. These are stress examples, not measured confidence intervals. Include weak collection and low output together.
- During analysis: add completed-investment and construction-delay cases. Retain the result that none meets both floors under base capture.
- During checking: include the full-capture capacity boundary and compare the analytic threshold with direct market solutions. Code uses floating-point tolerances of 1e-10 for floor comparisons.
