## Comparison and baseline

Compare four treatments of the same additional cash: no new payment; counted cash; a shared disregard; a proposed split treatment that disregards the payment for the upper test and expressly credits it to the lower income route. Keep other household income and other conditions fixed.

The no-payment baseline is mandatory. A shared disregard leaves both income inputs equal to that baseline: it does not itself take away an already-met route. Counted cash can open the lower route but cross the upper ceiling. A split treatment can avoid this arithmetic tradeoff, conditional on lawful federal authority and additional resources. Mechanical qualification is not demonstrated enrollment, access to care or a welfare ranking.
