# P1: Financial Profiles of Workers Most Vulnerable to Coronavirus-Related Earnings Loss in the Spring of 2020

Source: [Brooke Helppie-McFall and Joanne W. Hsu](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/files/2020093pap.pdf)

Version: FEDS 2020-093 working paper. Publication/update date: 2020-10-27.

Retrieved: 2026-10-11T21:27:55Z. Locator: PDF pp. 5–16; especially PDF p. 6, data and obligations.

Researcher summary and limited quote; not a full source snapshot.

The authors use SCF family finances to compare workers with different pandemic employment risks. They compare savings with income and housing/debt expenses. Thus, financial resilience after earnings loss and payment commitments are established subjects; this study does not introduce them.

Short source quote:

> we compare families’ savings levels to their monthly income
