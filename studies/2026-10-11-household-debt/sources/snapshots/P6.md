# P6: How Much Did the CARES Act Help Households Stay Afloat?

Source: [James Aylward, Elizabeth Laderman, Luiz E. Oliveira, Gladys Teng](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/economic-letter/2021/07/how-much-did-cares-act-help-households-stay-afloat/)

Version: FRBSF Economic Letter 2021-18. Publication/update date: 2021-07-06.

Retrieved: 2026-10-11T21:42:56Z. Locator: Measuring household resilience; data and definition paragraphs.

Researcher summary and limited quote; not a full source snapshot.

The article builds a SIPP sample of 10,613 household heads and spouses and combines nonemployment income with liquid assets to assess consumption resilience. It compares that resilience with and without CARES Act support. Thus, the income-loss, cash-support and asset-buffer framework predates this study.
