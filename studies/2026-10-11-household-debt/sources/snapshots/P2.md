Researcher summary and limited quote; not a full source snapshot.

The authors separate changes in near-term mortgage payments from changes in longer-term principal obligations. They use mortgage-modification variation to study default and consumption. This is causal evidence in its setting; the current static accounting test supplies no comparable causal identification.

Short source quote:

> reducing long-term obligations with no change in short-term payments
