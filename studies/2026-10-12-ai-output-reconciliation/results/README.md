# Numerical result tables

These files are generated by the independent public analysis. Units are in their column names. The 2023 real-value base uses each case's 2023 nominal AI GDP; the annual power normalization changes that base only at machine precision.

`cases.csv` has all 12 year-by-case rows, component values, nominal output, price ratios, Fisher terms, chained Laspeyres/Paasche/Fisher indexes, real levels and annual growth. `component-prices.csv` lists the five signed component price ratios for both transitions in each case. Chained Laspeyres and Paasche indexes are reported separately; their step factors define each Fisher step.

`training-reconciliation.csv` reports both training volume series with 2023 = 100. The operations-price index is a residual that makes price times volume equal nominal training value. It is not an estimate of pure rental-price change. `factorial-effects.csv` reports paired contrasts in real and nominal levels and in annual growth. A level difference in percent and a growth difference in percentage points are different quantities.

`power-ledger.csv` compares the source-generated energy with listed annual assumptions, the required scale factors, and both associated costs. Only listed power costs enter the four main cases. `source-comparison.csv` checks all 96 GDP output cells against the source-function adapter. `summary.json` records this comparison.

`printed-cell-checks.csv` and `printed-check-summary.json` retain all 54 distinct target cells and both direct-rounding failures. A claim that all printed cells match would be incorrect. The two failures concern the 2024 integer Fisher index and integer real-growth display, not the full-precision source-function comparison.

These are deterministic, conditional accounting results. They do not estimate actual welfare, taxable income, or the date when AI replaces all human work.
