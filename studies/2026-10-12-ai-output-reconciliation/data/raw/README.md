# Aggregate input ledger

These files contain numerical facts and original aggregate exports from C1, the frozen publisher replication ZIP. They contain no source implementation or cluster-level records. The source archive and code remain private. See `../../sources/manifest.csv` for its URL, retrieval time and rights record, and `../../logs/execution-run-01.json` for all retained source-member hashes.

- `annual-inputs.csv`: six rows, one per year and power rule. Compute spending and datacenter labor are annual US dollars; physical compute is annual bit operations; electricity is watt-hours. `released_generated_power_Wh` always identifies the source monthly rule, while `selected_power_Wh` identifies the power used in that row. Electricity price is dollars per kilowatt-hour. Listed power cost is used in all GDP cases. The separate generated-power cost is a transparent ledger entry only.
- `monthly-power.csv`: 72 rows, 36 months under each power rule. Each annual-preserving value is the corresponding source monthly value times listed annual watt-hours divided by the source-generated annual sum. There are no observed monthly loads here.
- `parameters.json`: the numerical source assumptions needed for this aggregate reconstruction. The price ratios, inference shares, markup, labor share and H100 conversion factor are copied as numerical facts; no source implementation is copied. The fixed 50 percent training share applies to both physical operations and nominal spending.
- `source-case-outputs.json`: 12 annual aggregate GDP rows from the source function, including the documented in-memory training-price adapter in bridge cases. Each key joins a power rule and a training-price rule. All monetary values are dollars.
- `source-control.json`: the original full-run spending, operations, QA growth, rounded summary indexes and the adapter's allocation check. The rounded summary indexes are controls, not analysis inputs.
- `printed-targets.csv`: manually transcribed target cells from B1 Tables 1–4, B2 and P2 Tables 1 and 9–14 where relevant. It records each locator and display format. Formula-only cells, dashes and irrelevant chip-sales/whole-US tables are excluded. Repeated cells in different source versions share one row. The two approximation-marked component deflators are compared at their displayed precision.

The raw source's estimates are inherited assumptions and modeled quantities. This package does not treat them as newly verified observations. Files retain full machine precision where available; apparent differences near 2023–2024 zero effects reflect floating-point arithmetic.
